# 3. TREMOR is finance-first; geopolitics is the signal

Date: 2026-11-18
Status: Accepted

## Context

TREMOR started as a geopolitical layer over live spatial data, with market links as one feature (ADR-0002). Its author plans to major in finance (fintech emphasis) with a minor in AI business, and wants TREMOR to be a portfolio piece that shows that combination.

## Decision

TREMOR is reframed as a **finance product**. Its purpose is to turn geopolitical Events into market-relevant insight about Assets. Geopolitics and sensor data are *inputs*; financial understanding is the *output*.

The core unit stays the **Event** (an occurrence at a place and time, involving Actors, linked to sensor data and Assets).

## Consequences

- Features are judged by whether they improve financial insight, not geopolitical coverage.
- **User and audience (settled):** TREMOR is built for a persona user, a self-directed investor or analyst (the author). It is deployed publicly and can be demoed in about two minutes: a live URL, a strong README, and one or two worked case studies. Portfolio reviewers are the audience for the demo, not the product's users.
- ADR-0001 and ADR-0002 still hold.
